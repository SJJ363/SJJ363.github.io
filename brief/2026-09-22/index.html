The Brief

AI push meets human friction and thin proof

Sep 22, 2026 · 140 stories · 61 sources

New AI tools launch while skepticism about real efficiency gains grows

What's happening

A wave of AI product launches hit the wire today: Marsh rolled out an AI placement platform for its London market and a broker workbench, an AI-native small commercial insurer expanded into California, and several other carriers and platforms shipped new AI-powered tools. But alongside the launches came pointed skepticism: one piece questioned why AI hasn't actually made insurance more efficient, and a former Microsoft security chief warned that employee apathy, not the technology itself, could stall the industry's AI plans. Separately, Snorkel AI closed a large funding round tied to AI infrastructure, and Zurich Kotak's new SME digital insurance product drew coverage across three outlets.

Why it matters

The gap between AI announcements and proven results is widening into its own storyline, shifting the conversation from 'what can AI do' to 'why isn't it working yet' and 'who inside the company is holding it back.' Watch whether more carriers start naming AI-specific leadership roles, like the new chief scientist appointment in today's leadership moves, as a response to that adoption gap.
