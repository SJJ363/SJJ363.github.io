Brokers and MGAs keep trading hands as AI settles in

Oct 5, 2026 · 140 stories · 43 sources

Broker and MGA deals continue while AI tools reach policyholders and workflows

What's happening

Consolidation is still the steady drumbeat: a UK specialist broker, a Midwestern agency group and a trucking MGA all changed hands, while Zurich's Beazley acquisition is now reported as complete. Alongside the deals, AI is moving from pitch to product, with a cyber insurer giving small-business policyholders an AI risk advisor and a major agency software vendor building AI directly into its core system. Reinsurance and capacity are also in play, including a large life reinsurance agreement and a new US financial lines platform from a specialty carrier.

Why it matters

Buyers are collecting specialist books and distribution, which gives them more data and more ways to sell, and AI features built into everyday tools reach far more users than standalone pilots do. Watch how the newly combined Zurich and Beazley operation integrates, and whether AI advisors for policyholders begin to change how often customers contact their brokers and carriers.
