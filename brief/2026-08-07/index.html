The Brief

Gallagher's Apollo buy anchors a busy M&A day

Aug 7, 2026 · 140 stories · 47 sources

Brokers keep buying, AI funding narrows, cat risk gets creative

What's happening

Consolidation kept moving with Gallagher picking up Canadian tenant-insurance broker Apollo and MGA dealmaking reaching trucking-focused HDVI, alongside a wave of quarterly results from carriers like HCI, Kingstone and Kemper. A fresh Gallagher Re read on funding found AI startups now capturing nearly all insurtech investment dollars, even as the disclosed rounds in today's batch stayed modest. On the risk side, insurers leaned on new structures to manage exposure: parametric cover reached thousands of Colombian smallholder farmers, life insurers kept building out Bermuda reinsurance ties, and Canopius trimmed Bermuda contracts on price while still growing profit.

Why it matters

The AI funding concentration Gallagher Re flagged means fewer, larger bets on automation-focused startups, while everything else in the sector competes for a shrinking slice of capital. Watch whether broker consolidation and MGA roll-ups keep absorbing smaller specialty players, and whether parametric and Bermuda-based risk transfer expand as insurers look for structures that don't rely on ordinary reinsurance capacity.
