The Brief

Brokers keep buying, insurers take equity bets

Aug 9, 2026 · 140 stories · 52 sources

more broker deals land, plus a stake-taking twist and a fresh raise

What's happening

Three more broker acquisitions landed - a Washington benefits agency, a confirmed HDVI deal, and a Canadian digital broker picked up by Gallagher - extending the consolidation wave. Mapfre took a near-40% stake in Spain's Tuio, a different move: buying influence in an insurtech rather than swallowing it whole. India's InRisk Labs raised $27 million, and Diesta linked up with J.P. Morgan's payments network to move insurance money globally, while Gradient AI rebranded and commentators debated whether AI will eventually replace agents.

Why it matters

The Mapfre-Tuio stake is worth watching as a template: incumbents buying partial ownership rather than full control could become a cheaper way to get insurtech exposure without an outright deal. Keep an eye on whether more carriers follow that path, and whether the agent-replacement debate turns into actual changes at brokerages rather than just commentary.
