Topic

Insurtech

967 stories · tracked since Aug 5, 2024 · latest Jul 31, 2026

Insurtech is the use of software and data to build, sell and administer insurance, from underwriting through claims.

The subject covers the whole population of companies applying technology to insurance rather than any one product: underwriting engines, claims automation, distribution platforms and policy administration systems, built either as standalone startups or as new lines inside established carriers and brokers. Capital moves in through venture rounds, acquisitions and strategic equity stakes, and increasingly through public listings, rather than one standard deal shape.

What matters now is less whether the technology works and more whether it can be run at a profit: technology-native insurers report combined ratios and net income like any carrier, and the modernization spend behind that shift is itself a recurring cost line, not a one-off project. Incumbents are responding by taking equity stakes in insurtechs rather than only partnering with them, folding distribution or underwriting technology directly onto the balance sheet.

Coverage here spans technology-native carriers publishing quarterly results alongside pure infrastructure vendors, agency and MGA financing, and inclusion-focused initiatives in emerging markets, rather than settling on one business model. The centre of gravity has moved from headline funding rounds toward earnings, IPOs and strategic stakes, and this category overlaps heavily with funding, claims and distribution coverage rather than standing apart from them.
