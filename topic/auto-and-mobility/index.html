Auto insurance and mobility

259 stories · tracked since Aug 5, 2024 · latest Nov 27, 2026

Auto insurance and mobility is cover for cars and fleets, plus the data and services around driving, such as telematics and claims repair. It spans personal, commercial and embedded cover.

How a motor policy is priced, sold and paid out

A driver or fleet operator buys cover from a carrier, or through a broker, agent or comparison site that sells on the carrier's behalf. The premium reflects the vehicle, the driver, where the car is kept and the claims history of similar risks. Telematics adds measured behaviour, such as braking, speed and phone use, from a phone app or plug-in device. After an accident, claims run through repairers, replacement-vehicle providers and accident management firms.

Why motor remains hard to price and hold

Motor is the highest-volume personal line, so small shifts in repair costs, parts prices, medical costs or litigation reach many policies quickly. Carriers must reprice as inflation moves, yet customers shop on price and switch readily, so new business leaks away. Driving data raises its own questions: drivers worry about how it is used, and insurers have to show that scores are fair and portable. Fraud, patents on claims tools and changing vehicles add further friction.

Where the activity sits in the coverage

The companies in the coverage are mostly app-based insurers, comparison and lead-generation sites, telematics providers and incumbent carriers adding digital tools. Early emphasis fell on launching cover state by state or country by country. More recent items turn to AI agents, quotes inside general-purpose chat assistants, data-led claims and commercial programmes. The subject often overlaps with partnerships, product launches, AI and automation, claims and underwriting, and funding.

Personal, commercial and embedded cover compared

Personal auto covers private drivers and is the most competitive and price-sensitive form. Commercial auto covers delivery vans, trucks, rideshare and fleets, where telematics and fleet dashboards inform underwriting and safety coaching. Embedded motor cover is sold inside another product, such as an electric vehicle purchase or a mobility app, so the customer meets the insurer at the point of use. Each has different buyers, data and distribution, though many firms work in more than one.
