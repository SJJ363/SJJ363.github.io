Auto insurance and mobility

205 stories · tracked since Aug 5, 2024 · latest Oct 8, 2026

Auto insurance and mobility covers insurance for cars and their drivers, plus the newer risks of connected, electric and self-driving vehicles. It spans personal motor policies, fleet cover and cover sold inside vehicle purchases.

How a motor policy is priced and sold

A driver or fleet owner pays a premium to an insurer, which covers liability to other people, damage to the vehicle and theft. Price rests on rating factors such as the driver's record, the vehicle, where it is kept and how much it is driven. Quotes come through direct carriers, brokers, comparison sites and agents. In many markets third-party cover is compulsory, which makes motor the largest everyday line for most carriers.

Why driving data and automation strain the old model

Telematics lets insurers price on observed behaviour, such as braking, speed and time of day, rather than proxies like age and postcode. That raises hard questions about privacy, consent and fairness, and drivers who agree to share data tend to be those who expect to benefit. Driver-assistance and self-driving systems move some fault from the person to the software. Liability then gets harder to assign, and repair costs rise as cars fill with sensors.

Where the activity sits today

Activity clusters around a few kinds of company: digital-first motor insurers, quote and comparison platforms, roadside and warranty providers, and carriers pairing with car makers. Product launches and partnerships dominate, with pricing and claims close behind. The emphasis has moved from selling cheaper quotes online toward behaviour-based pricing, vehicle-maker data and conversational channels, where assistants and AI agents begin the quote. Regulation and data protection keep surfacing alongside.

Embedded, electric and autonomous variants

Several varieties are easy to confuse. Behaviour-based cover prices from how a person drives. Embedded motor cover is offered at the point of buying a car, a ride or a financing product, often through a partner. Electric-vehicle cover has to deal with battery repair and charging risks. Autonomous-vehicle cover must decide who answers for a crash, and it is being shaped by manufacturers' own data. Markets differ in compulsion, takaful options and the role of local platforms.
