Parametric insurance companies

33 companies · 93 stories · $113.1M disclosed

Insurtech Daily tracks 33 companies working in parametric insurance — 18 insurtechs, 8 technology vendors and 3 insurers, across 93 stories going back to Sep 11, 2024.
