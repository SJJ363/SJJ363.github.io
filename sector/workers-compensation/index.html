Workers' compensation insurance companies

16 companies · 31 stories · $45.1M disclosed

Cover an employer carries for injuries to its own staff, priced on payroll and claims history and, increasingly, on workplace safety data. Insurtech Daily tracks 16 companies working in workers' compensation insurance — 6 insurtechs, 5 insurers and 2 brokers, across 31 stories going back to Jan 3, 2025. Between them they have disclosed $45.1M across 4 funding rounds, the largest CompScience's $27.6M. Every figure is compiled from the coverage on this page.
