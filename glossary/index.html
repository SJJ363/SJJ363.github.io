    Insurance broker

    An insurance broker arranges coverage for a policyholder, shopping terms across insurers rather than representing just one carrier.
  2. 25 stories

    MGA

    A managing general agent, or MGA, is a specialist firm an insurer authorizes to underwrite and bind policies on its behalf.
  3. 32 stories

    Reinsurance

    Reinsurance is insurance that insurers buy to shift part of the risk they have underwritten onto another carrier, for a share of the premium.
