What's happening

Acquisitions dominate the new stories, and this time the buyers are shopping for specific capabilities. Marsh picked up an agency, and Arch bought a dealer services business to widen its auto finance and insurance offering. Money is moving too: Collective raised $100 million, while a planned $304 million IPO was pulled after a regulatory inquiry, a reminder that public markets remain a harder door than private ones. Meanwhile, a PERILS estimate puts the Australian east coast storm loss at AU$1.6bn, and commentary keeps returning to AI agents running ahead of human oversight.

Why it matters

Buying niche distribution and specialty service businesses is how larger carriers and brokers lock in customers without building from scratch, so expect more small agency deals to follow. The IPO withdrawal suggests regulators are watching fast-growing financial firms closely, which could push more of them toward private funding. Watch whether insurers start setting formal controls for AI agents before a loss event forces the issue.
