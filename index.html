What's happening

Fresh funding is landing at the smaller end of the market. Canadian digital insurer PolicyMe raised C$30 million, and Spanish-based Weecover raised 2.3 million to expand across Europe. Alongside that, the talk about AI has shifted from pilots to operations. A monthly insurtech roundup points to agentic claims handling rising, and an Indian health insurer's CEO is describing AI that reaches beyond claims. Separately, Gradient AI launched an underwriting tool, and Amwins launched a clinical trials insurance product.

Why it matters

Money is going to firms that sell insurance directly or serve small businesses, which suggests investors still back distribution as well as software. As AI moves from claims into underwriting and customer service, expect the questions to shift from whether it works to who is accountable when it errs. Watch whether the next rounds follow this pattern and whether insurers begin to publish results from their agentic claims work.
