What's happening

Dealmaking moved from talk to signed paper this week: American Family agreed to buy Bowhead Specialty for $1.2 billion, Cover Genius picked up Friendsurance to push into European embedded banking, Mapfre took a stake in Tuio, and Ageas sold its Etiqa stake to Maybank. Alongside the deals, AI is showing up in actual customer-facing tools rather than strategy talk, with Plymouth Rock rolling out ChatGPT-powered home insurance quotes and insurers experimenting with agentic AI inside claims and BPO work. Cat and reinsurance pricing pressure continued, with wildfire detection tech launching in Spain and analysts warning pricing won't settle soon, while a cyber claim dispute reopened questions about war exclusions.

Why it matters

Specialty and embedded-insurance names are becoming the clearest acquisition targets, suggesting bigger carriers see more value buying distribution and niche underwriting than building it themselves. At the same time, AI tools reaching real customers raises the stakes on accuracy and oversight, and the cyber exclusion fight could reshape how insurers price and pay claims tied to critical infrastructure attacks.
