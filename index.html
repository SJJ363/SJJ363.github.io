What's happening

Acquisitions dominate the new stories, but this time the buyers are reaching for specialist capability rather than just scale: a management consultancy is adding a financial-services technology firm, a managing general agent is adding a reinsurance operation, and a Wisconsin agency is absorbing a local one. Alongside that, AI is the busiest theme, and it is showing up less as a product pitch than as a risk question, with Allianz Commercial warning that AI-related claims could hide a larger financial exposure. A new cover for AI computing hardware shows insurers starting to price that exposure rather than just discuss it. Meanwhile one carrier is ending new sales in a line of business, a reminder that not every move is expansion.

Why it matters

Consultancies and brokers buying technology and reinsurance skills suggests they expect clients to want help with both AI adoption and risk transfer from the same adviser. Watch whether insurers start writing explicit AI exclusions or dedicated AI cover as claims data builds, and whether more carriers follow the retreat from lines that no longer pay.
