What's happening

The newest stories show insurance moving into the chat window. Allianz Ireland is offering home insurance quotes through ChatGPT, and Atlas Risk Partners has added a ChatGPT insurance application, so a consumer can now start a policy inside an AI assistant instead of on a carrier site. Behind the scenes, AI is also being built into programs such as Aon and SAGE's precision crop insurance offering for US states. Dealmaking continues in parallel: AIG is taking Everest's Chile business, Rainier Partners bought Apex Insurance Services, and Markel is counting on Enstar's adverse development cover to give it runoff certainty. Outside the core industry, fintech lenders and wealth platforms are pulling in capital, with Wealthsimple and Lovey both announcing large raises or credit facilities.

Why it matters

If shoppers begin their search in an AI assistant, the assistant, not the carrier's website, becomes the front door, and carriers and brokers will compete to be the answer it surfaces. That raises questions about who owns the customer relationship and the data. Watch whether these chatbot channels produce bound policies rather than just quotes, and whether private equity keeps buying up brokerages and agencies as distribution changes shape.
