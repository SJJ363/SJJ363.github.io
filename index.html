What's happening

The newest stories point to a market where money is still moving, but in smaller pieces. Early-stage AI automation companies such as StitchStudio and Loti raised single-digit millions, while a trade report says insurtech funding fell 44.6% in the third quarter as mega-rounds cooled. At the other end, private equity firm Advent agreed to acquire insurance software provider Vermeg. Insurers also kept reporting growth, with Kin posting record new policy sales and Accelerant's revenue rising.

Why it matters

Smaller cheques and larger software buyouts suggest investors now prefer proven platforms or narrow tools with a clear job over big bets on unproven ones. That could leave established software vendors with more pull, and mean fewer insurtechs can raise on a story alone. Watch whether the Q3 drop in funding turns out to be a pause or a new normal, and whether more software providers become buyout targets.
