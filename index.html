Everything moving in insurtech.

Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

140 stories · 56 sources · updated Nov 26, 2026

What's happening

The new stories are led by insurtechs buying their way into new markets rather than building there. Igloo's purchase of Dubai-based Democrance, covered by four outlets, takes a Singapore-based player into the Middle East, and a UK broker deal and a Turkish digital insurance sale add to the pattern. Meanwhile agentic AI is showing up in specific workflows, with launches aimed at quoting and underwriting and at health insurance claims. Funding is thin by comparison, with one disclosed $6 million round for a disaster-recovery startup.

Why it matters

Buying a regional player gives a small insurtech licences, local partners and customers faster than entering a market from scratch, so expect more cross-border deals as growth slows at home. The AI launches are moving from demos to named workflows, which makes claims and underwriting outcomes the thing to measure next. A $120 million total for Indian insurtech funding, described as stable, hints that money is flowing toward fewer, more selective bets.
