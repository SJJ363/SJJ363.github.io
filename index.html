What's happening

The standout deal is Guidewire's reported plan to buy UK insurtech hyperexponential for up to $2.1bn, covered by three outlets, which shows core-system vendors paying heavily for pricing and modelling tools. Around it, AI is moving from pilots into specific jobs: a copilot for complex underwriting at PolicyMe, AI-driven crop pricing from Aon and SAGE, faster claims handling at Bdeo, and a real-time AI shopping gateway from Insurify. Smaller funding rounds, including CoverGo's $6m Series B1 and a $30m raise at Avante, show money still flowing to platforms aimed at rising insurance costs.

Why it matters

When a platform vendor buys a specialist, insurers that rely on either side may face new pricing, integration choices and fewer independent suppliers. Capital is also finding creative routes into insurance, from Hippo linking early-stage investment to reinsurance capacity with Greenlight Re to Barclays turning to insurers to hedge data center debt. Watch whether the Guidewire price becomes the benchmark for other underwriting-software targets, and whether the AI tools show measurable gains in loss ratios rather than just speed.
