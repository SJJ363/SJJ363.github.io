What's happening

Deal-making dominates today's wire: InsuranceDekho and RenewBuy are merging, Ignyte has bought InsureMyTrip, and Stewart is picking up ProTitleUSA and DocSolution, alongside a cluster of smaller product launches and partnership moves. AI keeps showing up on both sides of the ledger - Alice raised $140 million for AI safety and security work, and Rocket Money rolled out an AI financial assistant, even as a new report finds insurance employees increasingly anxious about AI's effect on their jobs. Property and cat-risk activity also ticked up, with Northern Re passing $1 billion in premium and Moody's flagging that record ILS capital is chasing riskier, harder-to-place perils.

Why it matters

The M&A cluster spans distinct niches - auto insurance distribution, title insurance and travel cover - suggesting consolidation pressure is broad rather than confined to one corner of the market. The gap between AI investment dollars and AI anxiety among rank-and-file staff is worth watching, since employee sentiment can slow adoption even when funding and executive appetite are strong.
