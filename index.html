What's happening

The new stories split between money and risk. Funding news leans toward debt rather than equity, with Thai insurtech Roojai securing a $35m facility from lender Lendable, reported by two outlets, alongside a smaller $25 million raise for Luminoah. Cyber is the other strong thread: estimates for losses from a Microsoft outage range widely, from $100M to $700M, and coverage points to a gap in cyber cover even as premiums climb. Regulators and insurers are also engaging with AI directly, as California's insurance department opens an AI oversight review and an MGA, Slice Insurance, builds ChatGPT and Claude into its operations.

Why it matters

Lenders funding growth through debt suggests some insurtechs are mature enough to borrow against their books rather than keep selling equity. The wide outage loss range shows how hard it still is to size a systemic cyber event, and buyers may find that what they thought was covered is not. Watch whether California's AI review sets a template other states copy, and whether AI use inside MGAs starts to draw the same scrutiny.
