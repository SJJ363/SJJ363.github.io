What's happening

A wave of AI-powered product launches hit the wire, from a cyber parametric tool covering cloud outage losses to a new AI-driven distribution platform, alongside an AI-focused claims automation acquisition. Broker and MGA consolidation kept moving with several smaller regional acquisitions layered on top of the week's larger buyout news. Why it matters

The shift from AI pilots to shipped products, especially in claims and distribution, suggests carriers and startups are past the experimentation phase and racing to get tools into production. Watch whether smaller acquirers keep snapping up niche automation capabilities rather than building them in-house, and whether profitable growth stories put pressure on still-unprofitable peers to show a path to breakeven.
