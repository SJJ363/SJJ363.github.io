What's happening

Marsh rolled out a large-scale property exchange aimed at data center and operational infrastructure risk, while a wave of product launches and partnerships showed carriers and MGAs stitching AI tools and specialty capacity into their offerings. Coverage of AI shifted from hype to practical questions: how agentic AI changes claims and underwriting workflows, why culture and process fixes matter more than the technology itself, and how cyber insurers are rewriting policies as AI agents misbehave. Separately, embedded insurance kept surfacing as a theme, with new data pointing to strong consumer appetite for it in auto and other lines.

Why it matters

The property exchange signals a push to build durable digital marketplaces for hard-to-place risk rather than one-off placements, which could reshape how large commercial risks get quoted and bound. On AI, the emphasis on process and culture over pure technology suggests the next competitive gap will be execution, not access to tools. Watch whether embedded insurance interest translates into more distribution partnerships, and whether cyber insurers' policy rewrites become a template other specialty lines follow.
