What's happening

The newest stories are about AI doing practical work rather than being promised. Sapiens and DXC are building AI into core insurance platforms, Cowbell is offering AI agents to policyholders, and Polygrade raised $6.3m to rebuild warranty claims with it. Claims is where much of this lands: Amica is widening its work with Flume, and Allianz finds the average business interruption claim is now 70% larger than property damage. Cyber is the other thread, with CFC adding cover for SME executives and a courts breach in Arizona raising questions about the state's cyber self-insurance fund.

Why it matters

As AI moves into the claims and policy workflow, the contest shifts from who has a demo to who can show reliable, explainable results. Liberty's CIO earning high marks for AI transparency hints at what buyers and regulators will ask for. Meanwhile bigger business interruption losses and public-sector cyber incidents put pressure on pricing and wording, so watch whether cyber cover for smaller firms and their executives keeps widening or tightens.
