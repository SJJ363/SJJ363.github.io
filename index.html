Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

131 stories · 49 sources · updated Nov 5, 2026

What's happening

Money is concentrating in a handful of AI-focused insurance startups. One underwriting platform was valued at $470 million after a $75 million Series B, and others raised $150 million and $43 million, some backed by big carriers and their venture arms. Yet the wider picture is weaker: Gallagher Re puts global insurtech funding in the third quarter at $1.36 billion, down 44.6%. Meanwhile, legacy balance sheets keep moving, with a $325 million loss portfolio transfer and a Swiss Re unit handing its South African book to Old Mutual Insure.

Why it matters

Investors appear to be paying up for AI tools that sit close to underwriting and claims, while backing fewer companies overall. That makes it harder for startups without a clear carrier-backed use case to raise money. Watch whether the next quarterly funding figures keep falling, and whether carrier-backed rounds become the main way young insurtechs get funded.
