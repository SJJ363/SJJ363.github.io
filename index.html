Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

140 stories · 57 sources · updated Oct 28, 2026

What's happening

The new stories split between carrier results and the tools built around them. Arch reported a $1 billion profit even as reinsurance premiums decline, while RenaissanceRe posted a $161 million loss, so the same softening market is landing very differently across the sector. Alongside that, AI is moving into everyday workflows: Travelers launched an AI quoting tool for workers compensation, Canopy Connect added email intake, and Verisk is bringing its insurance data into AI platforms. Smaller deals continued too, with Franklin Madison buying the insurtech Bindable and Aon's debt nearly doubling as its USI deal approaches close.

Why it matters

As premium rates ease, carriers will lean harder on automation and distribution to protect margins, and the gap between strong and weak results may widen. One new angle is the customer side: commentary warns that AI agents shopping nonstop could force carriers to defend every renewal. Cyber is the quieter risk to watch, with AM Best flagging software vulnerabilities as a possible systemic problem for cyber insurers.
