What's happening

Retail giants are stepping directly into insurance distribution: IKEA is teaming with Urban Jungle in the UK and Mapfre is partnering with Carrefour in Spain, both putting cover in front of shoppers rather than policyholders seeking it out. Broker and specialty consolidation kept up its pace, with Gallagher buying Apollo Insurance Solutions in Canada, AXIS picking up renewal rights to a DUAL North America book, and Inszone adding an Oklahoma agency. Capital markets kept financing newer risks too, with a Talcott sidecar backed by Goldman Sachs and Zurich structuring a dedicated reinsurance tower for data centre exposure. AI coverage split down the middle, with one piece questioning why agentic pilots are not paying off even as Lockton pushes its AI platform further across its US brokerage business.

Why it matters

Retail-embedded insurance gives non-insurance brands a new revenue line and could squeeze how independent brokers reach everyday customers. A dedicated reinsurance structure for data centres signals capital providers are starting to underwrite AI infrastructure itself as a distinct peril class worth watching as more of that buildout gets insured. The AI split is worth tracking too: which carriers move past pilot stalls into scaled deployment will separate real returns from hype.
