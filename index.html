Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

140 stories · 47 sources · updated Aug 11, 2026

What's happening

A fresh wave of consolidation hit the wire: Gallagher picked up two separate insurance brokers, Marsh McLennan Agency moved for Accel Holdings, and Cover Genius bought Berlin-based Friendsurance, alongside several smaller broker and reporting-platform deals. Property risk is drawing its own dealmaking now too, with FM acquiring wildfire-intelligence provider FortressFire to build structure-level cat modeling directly into underwriting. AI stories remain the largest single bucket, but the coverage has shifted from strategy talk to specific applications: agentic platforms, reconciliation workflows and customer-communication tools. Funding stayed modest apart from one standout headline putting insurtech investment at a four-year high.

Why it matters

The pace of broker roll-ups shows little sign of slowing, and cross-border deals like the Canadian and German acquisitions suggest buyers are widening their search beyond home markets. FM's wildfire-data purchase is a signal worth watching: carriers are starting to buy climate-science capability outright rather than license it, which could set a template for how insurers handle other peril types. If the funding rebound reported today holds up in the coming weeks, it would mark a real inflection after a stretch dominated almost entirely by M&A headlines.
