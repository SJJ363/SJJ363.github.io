What's happening

AI is showing up on both sides of the cyber conversation: insurers are rewriting cyber coverage because of AI-enabled attacks, and retailers heading into Black Friday are being warned about gaps in their protection. At the same time, reinsurers and carriers such as PartnerRe and New York Life are widening their use of AI in underwriting, while SCOR and Japan Post Insurance have won board approval for a reinsurance vehicle partnership. Pricing is moving the other way, with D&O premiums falling as competition intensifies, and executive surveys put cybercrime and AI investment at the top of the agenda.

Why it matters

When the same technology is both a new source of loss and a new tool for pricing it, the quality of an insurer's data and wording matters more than its marketing. Softer pricing in some lines could squeeze margins just as cyber exposures grow harder to model. Watch whether cyber policy language tightens around AI-driven incidents, and whether more reinsurers follow SCOR into dedicated risk-transfer vehicles.
