What's happening

Fresh coverage leans hard into AI, but the tone has shifted from adoption stories to caution: Brown & Brown says the real payoff is years away, Davies is warning that AI agents are amplifying conduct risk, and pieces on agentic AI and underwriter decision-making are wrestling with how much to trust the tools. Alongside that, dealmaking continues at a steady clip -- Mapfre taking a stake in Tuio, Cover Genius folding in Friendsurance to build out European embedded insurance, and Allianz backing Dili -- while embedded insurance also gets a fresh push in Southeast Asian retail.

Why it matters

The caution around AI marks a shift from 'how fast can we adopt this' to 'what does this break' -- conduct risk, delayed ROI, and labor economics are now part of the conversation, not just capability. Watch whether that skepticism slows the pace of AI-linked M&A and product launches, or whether embedded insurance's expansion into new retail markets keeps outrunning it.
