What's happening

A wave of new tie-ups shows insurers choosing to plug in AI rather than build it themselves: AXA is bringing in BytePlus to scale AI, Zurich is widening its use of WTW's Radar platform, and a new underwriting tool launched for SME business. Alongside that, cyber insurers are digesting what recent hacking incidents involving AI models mean for their books, and a debate is opening over whether relying on outside AI vendors leaves insurers exposed. Separate threads touched on AI's effect on jobs, with some insurers reportedly pulling back from automation tied to labor displacement, and on a fresh worry about risk concentrating in data centers.

Why it matters

Leaning on outside AI platforms speeds up deployment but shifts risk onto vendor relationships and shared infrastructure like data centers, which is exactly the kind of concentration risk now being flagged. Watch whether the build-versus-buy debate hardens into a real strategic split, and whether the AI-hacking and labor-displacement worries slow the partnership pace industry-wide.
