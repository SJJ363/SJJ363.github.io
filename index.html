What's happening

Fresh money is flowing to narrower, practical bets: Ancep raised $8.5 million for AI agents aimed at commercial brokers, Synctera raised $24.5 million for embedded finance, and IA Capital launched a fintech and insurtech fund backed by QBE. Insurers are also writing cover for the technology itself, with Cowbell launching standalone liability insurance for businesses that deploy AI agents. Elsewhere, Lemonade posted a $43.7 million third-quarter loss and Berkshire showed the steepest Q3 underwriting decline among US carriers, a reminder that profits are under pressure even as new products arrive.

Why it matters

Investors seem to favour tools that fit into existing broker and carrier workflows over big reinvention, and a dedicated fund from a major insurer suggests carriers want a direct stake in what gets built. Insuring AI agents is an early sign that the technology is becoming a risk category in its own right, so watch how pricing and exclusions develop. Also watch whether loss-making insurtechs like Lemonade can hold their funding appeal as underwriting results stay thin.
