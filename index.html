What's happening

The newest stories are dominated by AI, but mostly as everyday tooling rather than big bets: automating underwriting for small contractors, helping carriers win over agents, and even recruiting. Funding is small and focused on the back office, with roughly $6 million across two rounds, including a $3m raise to automate small contractor E&S underwriting and a $3m seed to fix insurance integration. Alongside that, carriers and brokers keep shipping niche products, from surety for data centers to parametric cover for GPU residual value, and a financial wellbeing platform from Gallagher.

Why it matters

Investors seem to be backing the unglamorous connective work, such as data handoffs and integrations, that lets AI actually plug into carriers. New products tied to data centers and GPUs show insurers pricing the physical footprint of the AI boom, while UK SMEs' growing interest in AI-related cyber cover suggests demand is arriving from smaller buyers too. Watch whether these small integration and underwriting tools win carrier pilots, and how fast specialty capacity follows the data-center risk.
