What's happening

Acquisitions are still the loudest signal, now reaching from a Wisconsin agency to a consulting firm and a reinsurance-focused buyer picking up a rival's assets. Alongside that, AI is moving from talking point to product, with a new underwriting tool, cover designed for GPU infrastructure, and a warning from Allianz Commercial that AI-related claims could hide larger financial exposure. Specialty launches continue too, including a clinical trials insurance product and a new underwriting venture, while funding stays small, with a single disclosed seed round of about $3 million.

Why it matters

Buyers appear to be paying for people, niche expertise and distribution rather than technology, which suggests consolidation will keep running ahead of venture money. As insurers write cover for AI hardware and tools, the harder question becomes how they measure the losses that could follow, so watch for how underwriters price and cap those risks. Also watch whether the broader fintech funding upswing starts to reach insurtech in larger rounds.
