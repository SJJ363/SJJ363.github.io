What's happening

The biggest move in these stories is ownership of the technology itself: Guidewire has agreed to buy hyperexponential, a UK maker of AI underwriting and pricing software, in a deal reported at up to $2.1 billion. Ardonagh is also folding its global MGA businesses under a single new brand, Orvia Underwriting, which brings about $3 billion of premium under one name. Elsewhere, AI is reaching further into daily operations, from agent search tools to a rate-shopping tool that could change how drivers compare quotes, while Slingshot's $27 million raise for AI-native embedded insurance was the main funding story.

Why it matters

When a core-system vendor buys a pricing specialist, underwriting models start to sit inside the platforms carriers already run, which could make them harder for rivals to avoid or replace. Chubb's chief executive saying the industry has to get its arms around AI liability, and CFC widening its AI coverage to healthcare and professional services, show that the risk side is growing in step with the tools. Watch whether the hyperexponential price sets a benchmark for other AI vendors, and whether AI shopping agents put new pressure on auto pricing and retention.
