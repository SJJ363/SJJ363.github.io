Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

What's happening

The new stories are dominated by money and capacity for specialty underwriters. Ledgebrook's $200m equity financing arrived with a multi-year Allianz reinsurance deal, Amwins set up a new specialty MGA, Aviva formally opened a US specialty business, and Zurich is backing a new haulage insurance launch. Brokers kept buying too, with Gallagher picking up an Irish MGA and Heffernan adding a Maryland brokerage. On the technology side, the talk has turned from simple tools to agentic systems, with Google and CoverGo outlining an agentic operating system for insurers and a new Sapiens index expecting fully autonomous AI within three years.

Why it matters

Pairing fresh equity with reinsurance support suggests backers now see specialty niches, not just software, as where returns are. As AI takes on more of the work, brokers are already asking whether it needs its own insurance market, so expect questions about who carries the risk when an autonomous system makes a mistake. Also watch the senators' push for claims data and the industry's shared data standards, since both could shape what insurers must disclose and how their systems connect.
