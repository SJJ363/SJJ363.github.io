What's happening

Insurance is showing up inside consumer chatbots: GEICO and Coverage Cat now offer quote estimates through ChatGPT. At the same time, much of the trade commentary asks how far carriers should trust AI, with calls for tighter carrier control of AI in core systems, better data, and a warning that AI agents that bind policies could leave insurers liable. Automation Anywhere's planned purchase of Boost.ai adds another AI vendor deal to the mix.

Why it matters

Quotes in a chat window are cheap to offer, but binding coverage by machine raises questions about who is responsible when an agent gets it wrong. Watch whether estimates stay estimates or move toward bound policies, and whether carriers or regulators set the rules first. Separately, Gallagher Re's report of a 44.6% drop in third-quarter insurtech funding suggests the money behind these tools is getting choosier.
