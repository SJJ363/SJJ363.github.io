What's happening

Capital keeps flowing into risk-bearing, with a new Lloyd's syndicate launched by Aon and Blackstone funds with QBE as managing agent, even as a JP Morgan note expects double-digit price declines in property catastrophe reinsurance to persist. On the technology side, AI is the thread through nearly half the new stories, but the angle has shifted from funding to adoption: Guardian is partnering with Sierra on AI customer service, an agent-software tool is being bought by Sierra, and vendors like Insurity are arguing carriers need tighter control over AI inside core systems. Smaller deals continue, including a Texas credit union's insurance operations moving to Confie and a major investor trimming its wefox stake over debt default risk.

Why it matters

Softer reinsurance pricing and fresh capacity tend to squeeze margins and push carriers to find efficiency elsewhere, which helps explain the appetite for AI in service and operations. The growing questions about data quality and carrier control suggest the next test is whether these tools can be trusted in core systems, not whether they can be bought. Watch whether cat pricing keeps falling into the next renewal season, and whether struggling insurtechs such as wefox face further pressure from backers.
