What's happening

AI is showing up less as pilots now and more as org-chart decisions, with carriers naming dedicated analytics and AI leaders to steer underwriting strategy. Alongside that, a wave of smaller launches and deals is reshaping distribution: a new cyber-focused brokerage entered the market, a startup program aims to let any company spin up a digital brokerage quickly, and several firms expanded or acquired regional operations. M&A activity stayed steady but modest, mostly regional agency buys and international footprint moves rather than blockbuster deals.

Why it matters

Putting AI oversight into named leadership roles signals carriers expect this to be a durable capability, not a side project, which should speed up how fast underwriting tools reach the field. The string of small brokerage and agency moves suggests distribution is fragmenting into more specialized, tech-enabled niches, so watch whether these startups can scale past their launch headlines into real premium volume.
