Funding, deals, launches and platform shifts — aggregated all day, from hundreds of outlets.

109 stories · 39 sources · updated Nov 11, 2026

What's happening

Dealmaking is crossing borders and sectors: an Austrian insurer is buying a Turkish health insurer, Marsh is picking up an investment consulting firm, and private equity is backing regional brokers such as King Risk in Florida. Fresh money is also flowing to the sector itself, with a QBE-backed fund for fintech and insurtech and a small $8 million round for an AI-focused startup, though the three disclosed rounds total only about $25 million. Meanwhile AI keeps showing up in the day-to-day work of underwriting and claims, from submission triage to the question of where human judgment still belongs. Product news leans toward bundling, as Tune Protect folds lifestyle services into its insurance and Tokio Marine extends robot insurance to Japan and the UK.

Why it matters

Buyers appear to want distribution, health books and advisory capabilities more than new technology, which suggests consolidation is shaping the market as much as startups are. A new corporate-backed fund gives early-stage insurtechs another source of capital, but the small round sizes show investors are still cautious. Watch AI lending: a surge in it is testing credit insurance capacity, and that could tighten cover or raise prices if banks all seek protection at once.
