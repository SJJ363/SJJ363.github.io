What's happening

Acquirers are buying narrow capabilities rather than whole platforms. Zocdoc made its first purchase in nearly 20 years with an insurance verification startup, Novacore is adding cross-border trucking cover through Diesel Insurance Solutions, and Porch Group is paying $200M for Allied Trust. Funding is small by comparison, with roughly $6 million disclosed across two rounds, including a seed for an AI startup and a £1m raise for a renewals offer. In the trade press the AI conversation has shifted from announcements to practice, with pieces on holding AI agents to human standards and on insurers urging regulators to keep pricing models out of AI rules.

Why it matters

Buying a specialist team or licence is quicker than building one, so expect more tuck-in deals from companies outside insurance and from carriers filling product gaps. The regulatory argument over whether pricing models count as AI could decide how much oversight underwriting automation faces. Watch whether the debate over telematics versus credit in auto pricing picks up the same scrutiny.
