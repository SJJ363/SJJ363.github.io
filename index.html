What's happening

The new stories are less about big deals and more about specialists getting set up. Small funding rounds, such as Nettle's $4.8 million for faster risk inspections and SecondSight's Series A, sit alongside new specialty vehicles, including an Amwins-launched MGA and extra London fine art capacity at Markel International. Cyber is drawing talent, with hires at Aviva Specialty and Aurenity, while a California rule on AI in hiring is prompting fresh questions about employment practices cover.

Why it matters

Money is flowing to narrow tools that fix a specific step in underwriting or inspection, and not to broad platforms. Experienced underwriters are moving to new or growing cyber and specialty units, so watch whether capacity follows them. Regulators setting limits on AI use could start to change how insurers price and write the liability cover that sits behind it.
