What's happening

The new stories are dominated by ownership changes and the money behind them. Aon has completed its $17 billion purchase of USI, Burns & Wilcox is buying Mercury Underwriters, and Zurich has lifted its estimate of savings from its Beazley deal to $1.8bn. Core Specialty has filed to list on the NYSE, with its private-equity backers reported to be seeking liquidity. Meanwhile, AI keeps appearing in everyday work, from document tools for mortgage brokers to claims strategies and reviews of MGA renewals.

Why it matters

Deals that were announced months ago are now closing, and a public listing is on the table for a PE-backed specialty carrier. That puts pressure on the integration work that has to deliver the promised savings. The PEMCO story points to a more careful buyer attitude on AI: partner with vendors, but avoid being locked in. Watch whether Core Specialty's filing opens the door for other PE-owned insurers, and how Morningstar DBRS's warning about data centre exposure shapes insurers' appetite for AI-related risk.
