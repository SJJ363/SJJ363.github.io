140 stories · 48 sources · updated Nov 24, 2026

What's happening

AI is turning up inside everyday insurance workflows rather than at the edges. Sixfold is bringing AI underwriting to a German MGA, and a small claims-automation insurtech has lifted its total funding to about $4.77 million. Consolidation is steady too: Applied Systems has bought the insurtech Tailwind, and BrokerLink has picked up two Alberta brokerages. Alongside that, carriers and brokers are launching niche covers, such as Marsh's cyber policy for airlines, which three outlets covered.

Why it matters

Smaller insurers and MGAs can now buy AI underwriting from vendors instead of building it, which narrows the gap with bigger rivals. Software makers buying insurtechs suggests the winners may be those who own both the workflow and the tooling. Watch for the cost of overclaiming, since Allianz is already warning that AI washing could be expensive for businesses.
