What's happening

Most of the new stories are about companies expanding what they already have. Alan bought Tanel to extend its health business into Africa, and Alliant added a New Hampshire brokerage. Launches are spread across lines: Lemonade opened renters cover in Hawaii, Oscar introduced an AI-powered health marketplace, and a new E&S carrier is aimed at coastal commercial property. Tune Protect is taking embedded travel cover into three more Southeast Asian countries, and Alan is also running a consumer campaign with a footballer.

Why it matters

Buying a local player or stepping into a new region is a faster way to add customers than building from scratch, and it suggests the strongest insurtechs now have the confidence to expand outward rather than just raise money. Some of the sharper commentary is about the risk side: one piece warns that technology can make bad underwriting faster, and a carrier tech chief says AI agents are already calling insurers. Watch whether the consumer-facing launches turn into measurable growth, and whether insurers begin setting rules for automated callers.
