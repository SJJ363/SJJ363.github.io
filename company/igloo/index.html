Igloo

16 stories · tracked since Aug 19, 2024 · last seen Jun 30, 2026

Igloo is a Singapore-based insurtech selling microinsurance and embedded cover across South-East Asia through telcos, retailers and platforms. It acts as a distributor and technology provider, not an insurer.
