Root

18 stories · tracked since Aug 29, 2024 · last seen Aug 3, 2026

Root is a US auto insurer that prices policies using telematics data collected from drivers' smartphones instead of traditional rating factors. It sells car insurance directly to consumers and through embedded partnerships with dealerships, fintech apps and other platforms.
