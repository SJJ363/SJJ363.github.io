Cover Genius

19 stories · tracked since Dec 4, 2024 · last seen Aug 4, 2026

Cover Genius is a global insurtech providing embedded insurance through an API used by airlines, e-commerce platforms and banks. It lets digital businesses offer protection products to customers at checkout or point of sale.
