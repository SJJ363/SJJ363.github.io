Kin

4 stories · tracked since Jan 22, 2025 · last seen Jul 29, 2026

Kin is a US insurtech that sells homeowners insurance directly to consumers, focusing on coastal and climate-exposed markets like Florida and California. It underwrites policies covering wildfire, flood and other catastrophe risks that many traditional insurers avoid in these regions.
