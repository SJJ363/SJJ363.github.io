Kayna

14 stories · tracked since Sep 11, 2024 · last seen Jul 16, 2026

Kayna is an Irish insurtech that embeds small-business insurance inside the vertical software platforms those businesses already use. It works with brokers and carriers rather than carrying risk itself.
