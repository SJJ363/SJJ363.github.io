Zopper

11 stories · tracked since Nov 21, 2024 · last seen Jun 18, 2025

Zopper is an Indian insurtech that provides embedded insurance distribution technology, letting brands and retailers sell insurance at the point of sale. It also runs an insurance broking business alongside its digital infrastructure platform.
