Lloyd's

8 stories · tracked since Aug 14, 2025 · last seen Aug 17, 2026

Lloyd's is the London-based insurance and reinsurance marketplace where syndicates backed by member capital underwrite specialty and commercial risks worldwide. It sets marketwide standards, licenses coverholders and MGAs, and runs an innovation accelerator for insurtechs.
