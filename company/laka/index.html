Laka

4 stories · tracked since Sep 18, 2024 · last seen Jul 15, 2025

Laka is a London-based insurer that provides collective, usage-based insurance for cyclists and bike retailers across Europe. It covers bicycles and e-bikes for individual riders as well as fleets sold through bike shops and manufacturers.
