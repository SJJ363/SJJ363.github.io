Acquirers Buy Automation, Pet Insurance Goes Digital

Software buyouts, small AI raises, and a gap between hype and readiness

What's happening

The newest stories are about who owns the tools. Applied Systems buying the insurtech Tailwind was picked up by four outlets, and smaller brokerage deals in the UK and Canada added to a steady run of acquisitions. At the same time, a handful of small AI-focused startups raised money, and pet insurance is moving onto comparison sites with an AI-built product. A survey suggests travel insurance will automate first and commercial lines last.

Why it matters

Software vendors are buying automation rather than waiting to build it, which pulls agencies and brokers further into a few large platforms. The survey and a warning that 'AI washing' could be costly point to a gap between what firms claim and what they can run in practice. Watch whether buyers start asking for proof of results, and whether insurers begin writing cover for AI-driven cyber attacks, which many retailers lack going into Black Friday.
